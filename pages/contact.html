Finding my office

The Paris Metro website is excellent for finding transport to ENS (24 rue Lhomond, red dot above). To reach my office, take the elevator marked C or D to the 2nd floor. I am in room E208 (green dot above).

When you see a long orange corridor, take it. There is an international manhunt for the person who picked this colour. But keep walking into the blinding orange light. Eventually you will hit a fork. And as Yogi Berra says, 'just take it'.
