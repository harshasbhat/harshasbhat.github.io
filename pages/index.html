Harsha S. Bhat

Directeur de Recherche, CNRS · Laboratoire de Géologie, École Normale Supérieure Professor, Laboratoire de Mécanique des Solides, École Polytechnique

I study how faults break, at the interface between earthquake physics and solid mechanics.

With occasional excursions into rock mechanics, tectonics and seismology. B.E. in Civil Engineering from NIT Karnataka, Ph.D. from Harvard University, postdoctoral work at Caltech and the University of Southern California.
