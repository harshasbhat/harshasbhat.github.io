I am a senior CNRS Research Scientist (Directeur de Recherche) working at the Laboratoire de Géologie in École Normale Supérieure and a Professor at the Laboratoire de Mécanique des Solides, École Polytechnique.

I work mainly at the interface between earthquake physics and solid mechanics, making occasional excursions into rock mechanics, tectonics and seismology.

I am a solid mechanician and earthquake source physicist by training. I obtained my B.E. in Civil Engineering from Karnataka Regional Engineering College in India (now the National Institute of Technology Karnataka, Surathkal). I then obtained my Ph.D. from Harvard University, under the supervision of Prof. James R. Rice and Dr. Renata Dmowska. I did my postdoctoral studies jointly at Caltech and the University of Southern California, working with Prof. Ares J. Rosakis and Prof. Charles G. Sammis.

I joined CNRS in January 2012 as a Grade 1 Research Scientist and worked at the Institut de Physique du Globe de Paris (IPGP) until April 2016.

I have been awarded the Prix Michel Gouilloud Schlumberger from the Académie des Sciences; you can watch the ceremony. I was also awarded a European Research Council Consolidator Grant of €2.0M for my project PERSISMO.
